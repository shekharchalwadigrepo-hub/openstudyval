# openstudyval 0.1.0

* Initial CRAN-candidate release.
* Submission validator for a study folder (CSV, RDS, and SAS XPORT when haven is installed).
* Engines for SDTM, SEND, and ADaM structural checks, XPORT constraints, and FDA eCTD technical-rejection criteria 1734, 1735, 1736, and 1738.
* Optional define.xml cross-check when xml2 is installed.
* Extensible rule catalog (CSV) and HTML/CSV validation report.
* Independent implementation. Not affiliated with Pinnacle 21 or the FDA.
